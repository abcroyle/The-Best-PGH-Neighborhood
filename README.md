# The-Best-PGH-Neighborhood
Our team analyzed several metrics to determine the "best" neighborhood in Pittsburgh, Pennsylvania.

To determine what makes a good neighborhood, our team referred to Figure 4 in ["Multidimensional Attributes of Neighbourhood Quality: A Systematic Review"](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10687291/#sec2) (Ozgur Gocer, Yuan Wei, Ayse Ozbil Torun, Seraphim Alvanides, Christhina Candido), which identified the most frequently referred to terms in regard to neighborhood quality. Out of urban green and blue, amenities, housing quality, accessibility and urban mobility, physical environment, and socio-economic environment, our team chose to analyze urban green and blue, amenities, and the socio-economic environment of different neighborhoods within Pittsburgh.

Using these metrics, we determined the "best" neighborhood in Pittsburgh.
